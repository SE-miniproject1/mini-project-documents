# Sprint demo videos

Both sprint demonstration videos are stored in this repository.

## Sprint 1

[Watch the Sprint 1 demo](demo/sprint1-demo.mp4) (75 seconds, 1280 × 720, captioned, no sound).

The recording demonstrates staff sign-in and role-based navigation, inventory availability, donor search and eligibility, recording a donation, the barcode label, screening-result checks and release, live stock counts, and a donor's restricted view.

## Sprint 2

[Watch the Sprint 2 demo](demo/sprint2-demo.mp4).

The recording demonstrates the Sprint 2 workflows implemented for the project. Refer to the current application and the Sprint 2 acceptance criteria when reviewing the demonstration.

## Recording safety

The videos are demonstration artifacts containing seeded project data. They are not intended to show real donor or patient information. Do not place real credentials, personal data, or production records in demonstration recordings.
